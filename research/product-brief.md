# Product brief

## Product thesis
A premium-feeling mobile action game rooted in the epic scale and moral conflicts of the Mahabharata, with a cinematic solo campaign as the onboarding and progression layer and 1v1 combat as the repeatable competitive core.

**Working title:** ASTRA: Rise of the Legendary Warriors. Treat as provisional until trademark, cultural, legal, and store-name checks are complete.

## Target experience
1. Player enters a high-quality third-person story mission with Hindi or English presentation.
2. Mission teaches movement, dodge/parry, ranged/melee combat, and a character-specific power.
3. Completing objectives and boss encounters earns progression materials and unlocks combat options.
4. Player tests the learned skill in fair 1v1 matchmaking.
5. Ranked play, social features, 2v2, and survival modes are considered only after core retention and netcode are validated.

## Design pillars
- **Readable combat:** clear telegraphs, responsive controls, generous mobile UX, and reliable hit feedback.
- **Mastery over spending:** paid items should be cosmetic or convenience-oriented; no paid competitive stat advantage.
- **Narrative respect:** contextualize epic characters and events carefully; seek cultural advisors and legal review, avoid presenting contested interpretations as canonical fact.
- **Mobile performance:** scalable visuals and stable frame pacing on representative mid-range Android devices.
- **Trust:** transparent monetization, age-appropriate privacy, secure accounts, and clear matchmaking rules.

## Vertical-slice scope
- One visually distinctive mission environment.
- Two playable heroes with differentiated combat identities.
- One boss encounter and a small progression/reward loop.
- Hindi and English text; evaluate voice-over scope separately.
- Training arena and an online 1v1 feasibility spike.
- Analytics events for tutorial completion, mission completion, combat outcomes, crashes, and performance.
- Device matrix, accessibility pass, and playtest feedback.

## Out of scope for first prototype
Battle royale, 2v2 production, large open world, extensive hero roster, player trading, blockchain/NFT features, and large-scale live-ops. These add cost before the central combat loop is validated.

## Prototype budget discipline
Target ceiling: ₹10 lakh. Obtain quotes before committing to art, animation, voice, networking, or external QA. Maintain a contingency reserve and track actuals by deliverable. The earlier ₹9.90 lakh concept is a planning hypothesis, not a vendor quote or verified cost estimate.

## Success criteria (set baselines during prototype)
- New players can complete the tutorial without facilitator help.
- Players can explain why they won or lost a duel.
- Combat remains responsive under target-device load.
- 1v1 session completion and rematch intent are strong in moderated tests.
- No critical crash, exploit, or data-loss defects in the test build.
- Cost and delivery estimates are updated from real quotes and measured work, not assumptions.
