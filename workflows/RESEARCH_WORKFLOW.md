# Research workflow

1. **Frame the decision.** Write the decision, audience, constraints, and what evidence would change the recommendation.
2. **Search primary sources first.** Prefer official game/store pages, developer interviews, investor reports, platform documentation, and official policy/legal sources.
3. **Triangulate.** For market or player-behavior claims, seek multiple independent sources. Separate observed data from estimates and opinions.
4. **Log evidence.** Use `research/sources.md` with title, publisher, URL, publication date, access date, claim supported, and confidence.
5. **Avoid false precision.** Label estimates and unknowns; do not turn store ratings or a small interview sample into proof of revenue or retention.
6. **Convert findings into tests.** Every major recommendation should map to a prototype test, measurable success criterion, or explicit risk.
7. **Update decisions.** Maintain a short decision log: decision, evidence, alternatives, owner, date, and review trigger.
8. **Review for rights and sensitivity.** Verify title/art/music/character rights and use informed cultural consultation for Mahabharata-related narrative choices.

## Deliverable template
- Executive conclusion
- Decision to be made
- Evidence and source links
- What is known vs estimated vs unknown
- Risks and counter-evidence
- Recommended experiment
- Success/failure threshold
- Date to revisit
